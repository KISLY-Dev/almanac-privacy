1. Publisher and scope

The publisher's public name is KISLYDev. The publisher and personal data controller, for the publisher's processing described here, is Князев Илья Сергеевич, an individual registered as self-employed in the Russian Federation. Single contact for support and personal data enquiries: kisly_dev@mail.ru (Mail.ru). Effective date: 13 November 2026.

This policy covers the Android app “Альманах настольных игр” (Board Games Almanac), enquiries to the publisher and this policy's website. The first distribution store is RuStore, in all countries selected for release where it is available. Distribution through Google Play and other stores is planned for the future; actual additions and changes to processing will be reflected in this policy. A store's own data processing is governed by its documents.

The Russian and English editions are intended for a separate public GitHub Pages site and have the same meaning. This policy explains data processing and does not itself constitute consent to it.

2. Local data and offline use

The device stores saved games and move history where supported, game settings, favourites, the last opened game and the selected theme. The app also stores the access expiry, technical time and device reboot markers, reward confirmation flags and random operational tokens. These support game recovery and prevent the same reward being credited twice; they are not a developer advertising identifier.

No registration or user account is required. The developer has no proprietary cloud game server or cloud synchronisation. The advertising request code does not include saved games, favourites or the access record. This does not mean that third-party components do not process technical data.

The first launch grants 24 hours of general access. Each confirmed voluntary advertising reward adds exactly 6 hours to the later of the existing expiry and confirmation time. This period runs while the app is closed. Three games are free each week; the selection changes on Monday at 00:00 Moscow time and is calculated on the device. Free games and previously granted access work offline without a required ad view. Expiry does not delete saved games or itself close an already open game in the running app. Admission is checked again on the next entry.

Data is held in the app's private Android storage. Local recovery copies of damaged saves may remain. No automatic calendar retention period is set for them. Android backup is disabled in the app configuration; the access record is also excluded from standard backup. Device-to-device transfer may depend on Android and the manufacturer. The developer provides no cloud recovery service.

3. Yandex Mobile Ads

The app includes Yandex Mobile Ads and technical AppMetrica components. Rewarded advertising is offered only at the user's voluntary request, if an ad can be shown. An internet connection and an available ad are needed to extend access through advertising; availability and a reward are not guaranteed. Declining advertising does not delete saved games, previously credited time or access to the three free games of the week.

The publisher does not intend to provide personalised advertising. At startup, the SDK receives restrictive signals indicating no consent and an age restriction; optional App Ad Analytics and location tracking are disabled. Google Advertising ID permission (AD_ID) is excluded from the app manifest. The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera or microphone and does not collect birth dates. The absence of AD_ID permission does not establish the absence of all identifiers on every Android version. Tapping the ad button does not itself constitute consent to every kind of data processing.

These measures do not guarantee the absence of network traffic. The SDK and advertising infrastructure may process IP addresses and other network metadata, device, operating system and app information, available technical identifiers, and ad request, display and interaction events for ad delivery, technical operation, measurement and abuse prevention. SDK data categories are described in the official Yandex Mobile Ads information (in Russian); its table for default settings does not mean that every listed category is transmitted in our configuration. The exact scope depends on the version, platform, region and ad. The absence of all SDK network activity before an ad request is not guaranteed.

The publisher is responsible for integrating and configuring the advertising component and for lawful processing within the publisher's responsibility. Yandex carries out its own processing of advertising data; the relevant entities, purposes, recipients, retention and international transfers are described in its Privacy Policy. Linking to it does not relieve the publisher of their obligations. The publisher does not set or promise a separate fixed retention period for advertising data. Clearing the app does not delete information already sent to Yandex.

4. Voluntary enquiries

When you email the support address, the publisher receives your sender address, name if provided, message, date and technical email information, and any information you choose to attach. These are used to reply, resolve the issue and address data requests. Please do not send passwords, advertising identifiers, passport details or other unnecessary information. These details are not required for an ordinary enquiry; an email reply is impossible without a return address.

Mail.ru (the Mail service, VK LLC) is used to receive and send email. The provider processes messages and technical information to operate and secure its email service under its Mail-specific privacy terms and general Mail privacy policy (both in Russian). Its own processing is distinct from the publisher's handling of enquiries. Support email is not forwarded to Gmail. The publisher's administrative Gmail is used only for the RuStore and Yandex Advertising Network accounts, rather than user support.

The publisher retains correspondence for 12 months after the last enquiry, then deletes it from the email folders and working copies used by the publisher. A justified deletion request may shorten this period. Longer retention is permitted only to the extent and for the period necessary under law or for justified protection of legal rights; any refusal to delete will explain the applicable ground. This period is not a promise to delete all operational and backup records held by the email provider. The publisher and the email provider have access within the operation of the service; necessary information may be provided to competent authorities upon a lawful requirement.

5. Deletion, rights and security

Local data can be removed in Android settings by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. This removes local games, settings, recovery copies and the access record; recovery by the developer is not provided. Access expiry does not perform this deletion. Individual changes can also be made using the relevant game's functions.

For access, a copy of your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objections or withdrawal of consent where provided by law, contact the support email. State which right you wish to exercise and provide enough information to locate your enquiry. To protect others' data, the publisher may request proportionate verification of identity or a representative's authority; a passport copy is not automatically required for every email. The publisher handles requests within applicable statutory time limits and explains the outcome or lawful ground for refusal. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing or stop processing for which another lawful ground remains. Local game data is not automatically accessible to the publisher.

Where provided by law, you may contact Roskomnadzor, another competent supervisory authority where you are located, or a court.

For advertising service data, follow sections 9, “Your rights”, and 12, “Questions and Suggestions”, of Yandex's official policy, which lists current contacts for the relevant country. Yandex ID tools apply where data is linked to such an account; the app does not require you to create one. You can also ask the publisher to assist in directing your request. The publisher cannot promise to delete data directly from Yandex systems or identify whose data it is without sufficient information. For Mail.ru's and GitHub's own processing, use the contacts in their policies linked here.

Private Android storage and integrity checks reduce risks but do not guarantee absolute security or special encryption of all saves. Developer diagnostics are not intended to transmit saved game contents; Android and SDK mechanisms may operate when third-party failures are investigated.

6. Minors

The first release has a 12+ age category. This describes game content and does not by itself determine whether advertising or processing minors' data is lawful. The restrictive SDK age signal applies to all users and does not verify age or establish parental consent. The app does not provide a separate parental consent verification mechanism. Where such consent is required by applicable law, this policy and age rating do not replace it. A legal representative can contact the support email about a child's data. This policy does not guarantee compliance with the GDPR, COPPA or the laws of every country.

7. Legal grounds and international transfers

Local storage of games, settings and access records is needed to provide the features requested by the user. The existence of local records does not mean that the publisher receives them or that all technical data can be considered anonymous.

Within applicable law, the publisher's grounds for processing are distinguished by purpose: providing app features and necessary support for their use — performance of the relationship with the user; answering other voluntary enquiries and justified protection of legal rights — legitimate interests subject to the user's rights; handling lawful personal data requests — compliance with the relevant legal duties. If specific processing requires consent, it must be separate and meet the requirements of applicable law. Sending an email, using the app or requesting an ad does not amount to general consent to all processing. Declining advertising does not waive statutory rights to support and data protection.

Restrictive advertising settings do not replace a legal ground for actual SDK processing. This policy does not describe obtaining advertising or parental consent as an already implemented feature. Yandex, Mail.ru and GitHub explain the grounds for their own processing in their documents; this does not transfer all the publisher's duties to them.

Processing and international transfer requirements depend on the country and the particular operation. Yandex and Mail.ru may involve recipients under their terms; GitHub states that it processes data in the United States and other countries. This policy does not promise that all data stays in the user's country. Applicable localisation, legal ground and international transfer protection requirements still apply; mentioning foreign hosting in a policy does not itself make it lawful.

8. Policy website and changes

A separate GitHub Pages site is intended to host the Russian and English editions. The publisher does not add analytics, forms, advertising counters, external trackers or app source code to it. When GitHub Pages is visited, GitHub stores the visitor's IP address for security purposes, including without signing in: see the GitHub Pages description. Other technical request information, recipients, retention, international processing and contacts for requests are described in GitHub's privacy statement. The publisher does not set GitHub's log retention period or promise to delete its logs by clearing the app. External websites' rules apply when following their links.

Changes are published with a new effective date in both language editions at the same time. Where the law requires separate notice or consent, replacing the policy text alone is insufficient. Questions can be sent to the support email in section 1.
